# Vertical farming in Kuwait and Riyadh

Who grows food indoors in the two cities the Phase 4 vertical module is built for. Collected 2026-10-15 from news, trade press and company pages. Organizations only, no private individuals.

**Read before using:** almost every capacity figure below is the company's own claim, repeated by trade press. Most coverage is from 2020 to early 2025, so "status" is the last thing a source confirmed, not today's reality. Check each operator's own site before quoting it.

"Vertical" means stacked layers under LEDs. Some entries are aeroponic or hydroponic greenhouses instead, and they are marked that way, because the planner treats them as a different option.

## What this means for the planner

- The two cities have one big stacked-LED farm each (&ever Kuwait, VFCo Riyadh). Both sell leafy greens through supermarkets, so a small newcomer competes with them on price for lettuce. That matches the "why vertical farms failed" lesson: commodity greens are the hard market.
- The smaller players sell microgreens and herbs to restaurants and diet centers. This is the realistic small-scale option the planner should suggest first.
- Kuwait's newest capacity is aeroponic greenhouses, not stacked LED farms. The planner should show the greenhouse option next to the vertical one, with energy cost as the deciding number.
